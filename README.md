# Oops
A game about a crow attempting to save his daughter's balloon from flying away or popping by jumping on clouds







##  [Play Oops! on Itch.io](https://inzpire.itch.io/oops)

## Quickstart
- Click the Button above to open the game
- Press **Run Game** to play the game

## Controls
- Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move
- Click to shoot in the direction that your mouse is at
- Collect ammo on clouds for shootin
- Shoot at the obstacles that threaten the balloon's health
- The bar on the right of the player is ammo bar, if it runs out you can't shoot

## Features
- Short, sweet gameplay
- Juicy particles ands sfx
- Many obstacles
- A deep, totally touching story
